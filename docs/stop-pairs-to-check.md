# Stops that may be the same place

Found on 2026-11-28 in a copy of the live bus data (707 stops). These pairs are two names where one is
the other plus "Town", "Bus Stand", "Station", "More" or "Bazar". **Some are the same place, some are
not** (a town and its railway station can be different stops). Only the owner can decide.

To join a pair: **Admin > Bus Stops**, find the stop with fewer buses, tap the **Same place as...**
button (two arrows joining), pick the stop to keep, then **Join**. Its buses move to the kept stop and
its name stays as another name, so searching either name finds every bus. This can't be undone.
