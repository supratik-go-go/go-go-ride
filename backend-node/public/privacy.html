Privacy Policy

GO-GO Ride · Last updated 3 November 2026

GO-GO Ride is a local transport app for West Bengal. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and mobile number, and your email address and profile photo if you add them.
  • Using the app: your bookings, bus alarms, favourites, recent searches, reviews and support requests.
  • Bus and route information you send us: the details you submit, so our team can check them. Bus owners may also upload ownership documents, which are used only for verification.
  • Location: only when you ask for it, such as Use my location or Nearby Bus Stops. It is used to find stops near you and is not saved to your account.
  • Sign-in history: the device and IP address of each sign-in, so you can spot unfamiliar access.

How we use it

We use this information to run your account, show bus and route information, send alarms and notifications, handle bookings and support requests, verify listings, and keep the service safe. We do not sell your personal information and the app does not show advertising.

Who else is involved

  • Hosting: our server and database are hosted by Hostinger.
  • Maps: map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap, which can see your device's IP address when they load.
  • Daily News: tapping a headline opens the publisher's website, where their own privacy policy applies.
  • Drivers and bus owners: if you call a driver or a bus operator, they will see your phone number.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. Your name, phone number, email, photo, alarms, favourites, searches, reviews, notifications and sign-in history are removed straight away. Booking and support records are kept without your name or contact details where we need them for our records. Bus owners and drivers must remove their listings first.

If you can no longer open the app, contact us using the details below and we will delete your account for you.

Children

GO-GO Ride is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
